# Explicit Consent for the Processing of Personal Data (KVKK / GDPR)

**Last Updated:** June 29, 2026

Under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), I hereby declare that I have been informed in detail beforehand via the Privacy Policy regarding the processing of my personal data by **Longerix Labs** in connection with the **LensEat** application ("App").

By accepting this Explicit Consent Text, I give my free, specific, and informed consent for the following data processing activities necessary for using the App’s services, generating personalized recommendations, and running the AI-based meal analysis features:

1. **Processing of Sensitive Personal Data:**
   The processing of my **sensitive personal data (such as health, diet, weight, height, and physiological measurements)** entered by myself or synchronized from integrated health apps (Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect) to generate personalized nutrition analysis and goals,

2. **Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Data:**
   The transfer of my sensitive personal data, profile information, and uploaded meal photos to **servers located abroad (specifically in the United States)**, where the App's database infrastructure (Google Firebase) and AI processing systems (Anthropic Claude AI) are securely hosted,

3. **Processing of Visual Data via AI:**
   The processing of uploaded meal photos through artificial intelligence models to scan, identify, and perform nutritional analysis of the food.

I understand that I can withdraw this consent at any time by permanently deleting my account and data via the **Settings > Delete Account** section in the App, or by contacting the support team at **longerixlabs@gmail.com**.

## 6. Data Subject Rights
Under KVKK and GDPR, you have the following rights:
- **Access:** Confirm whether your data is being processed.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure / Deletion:** Request deletion of your data.
- **Restriction of Processing:** Request that processing be halted.
- **Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine‑readable format (JSON).
- **Objection:** Object to processing activities.

## 7. Data Security Measures
Your data is protected with SSL/TLS encryption in transit and at‑rest encryption on Firebase servers. Strict access controls are enforced to prevent unauthorized access.

## 8. Data Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals **within 72 hours** as required by GDPR, and as soon as possible under KVKK.

## 9. Automated Decision‑Making and Profiling
