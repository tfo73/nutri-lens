# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 29, 2026

We at Nutri Lens ("app", "we", "us", or "our") value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our application.

## 1. Data Collected and Purpose of Use

In order for our app to function, we collect certain personal and device data:
- **Camera and Photos:** Access to the camera and photo library is requested to scan meals and perform nutritional analysis. These photos are processed solely for real-time analysis.
- **Microphone:** Used when you want to input meals via voice commands.
- **Health Data (Apple HealthKit & Google Health Connect):** Your daily steps, exercises, and calories burned are synchronized with the app. This data is **strictly NOT used for advertising or marketing purposes, nor is it sold to third parties**. It is used exclusively to calculate your in-app goals.
- **Account Information:** Using Firebase, basic profile information such as your email address and name is created and stored securely.

## 2. Third-Party Services (AI and Analytics)

To provide you with a better service, we share data with certain third-party providers:
- **Anthropic (Claude AI):** The meal photos you upload or texts you enter are securely sent to the Anthropic API for calorie and macro analysis. No personally identifiable information (such as your name or email) is sent alongside the photos.
- **Firebase:** App infrastructure, database (Firestore), and user authentication are provided by Google Firebase.
- **OpenFoodFacts:** Barcode numbers are queried against the OpenFoodFacts database for barcode scanning operations.

## 3. Data Security and Deletion

The security of your personal data is important to us. Your data is stored encrypted within the Firebase infrastructure.
Users can permanently delete their account and all associated data (photos, health records, profile information) at any time from the **Settings > Delete Account** section within the app.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Nutri Lens is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13. If you become aware that data has been collected from your child without your consent, please contact us.

## 5. Contact

For any questions or requests regarding our privacy policy or your data, you can contact us via the in-app feedback section or directly through our email address.
